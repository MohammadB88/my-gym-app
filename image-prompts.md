# Exercise Image Prompts — Day 1

Prompts for generating instructional exercise photos for Day 1 of the training plan.

**Shared style (applies to every prompt below):** Photorealistic landscape photo (16:9), clean modern gym, bright even natural lighting with soft daylight from large windows, shallow depth of field, neutral athletic clothing with no logos, encouraging professional fitness-instruction aesthetic. Ultra-detailed, high resolution, anatomically correct hands and body proportions, realistic muscle definition. Correct, safe textbook form.

**Shared negative / avoid (append to every prompt):** distorted or extra fingers, extra limbs, text, watermarks, logos, cartoon or 3D-render look, cluttered background, distracting bystanders.

Target files live in `images/` with the filenames shown in each heading.

---

## 01 — Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown Machine
`images/01-lat-pulldown.jpg`

**Prompt:** A fit adult performing a neutral-grip lat pulldown on a selectorized cable machine. Seated with thighs secured under the knee pad, gripping a narrow neutral-grip (palms-facing) attachment, arms extended overhead at the top of the movement, pulling the bar down toward the upper chest — not behind the neck. Torso upright with only a slight, controlled backward lean; ribs down, shoulder blades drawing down and back; visible lat and mid-back engagement. Side-front three-quarter camera angle so the range of motion, neutral grip, and seated posture are all clearly visible. Weight stack softly visible in the background.

**Avoid also:** bar pulled behind the neck, excessive backward lean or arching, wide pronated grip, standing position.

## 02 — Machine Chest Press
`images/02-chest-press.jpg`

**Prompt:** A fit adult performing a seated machine chest press. Seated with back flat against the pad, handles set near mid-chest, gripping the handles and pressing them forward to near full extension without locking out hard. Shoulder blades gently set down and back against the pad, chest up, forearms roughly parallel to the floor. Side three-quarter camera angle showing the horizontal press path and chest/triceps engagement. Weight stack softly visible in the background.

**Avoid also:** flared shoulders lifting off the pad, handles set too high or too low, arched lower back, forceful elbow lockout.

---

## 03 — Hip Thrust / Glute Bridge Machine
`images/03-hip-thrust.jpg`

**Prompt:** A fit adult performing a hip thrust on a dedicated hip-thrust machine (or glute-bridge machine). Upper back supported on the bench pad, padded lever or belt across the hips, feet planted flat and shoulder-width, driving through the heels to extend the hips to a neutral, level lockout with glutes squeezed — torso and thighs forming a straight line, shins vertical. Ribs down, no excessive lower-back arch. Side camera angle showing full hip extension and the straight torso-to-thigh line.

**Avoid also:** overextended or arched lower back at the top, feet too far forward, chin tucked hard, hyperextended lockout.

---

## 04 — Lying Leg Curl
`images/04-lying-leg-curl.jpg`

**Prompt:** A fit adult performing a lying leg curl on a prone leg-curl machine. Lying face-down on the pad, ankle pad resting just above the heels, curling the heels smoothly toward the glutes through a controlled, pain-free range. Knee aligned with the machine pivot, hips staying flat and pressed into the pad — not lifting. Side camera angle showing the knee-flexion path and hamstring engagement.

**Avoid also:** hips lifting off the pad, jerky or bouncing motion, knee misaligned with the pivot, hyperextended range.

## 05 — Reverse Pec Deck / Rear-Delt Machine
`images/05-reverse-pec-deck.jpg`

**Prompt:** A fit adult performing a reverse pec deck (rear-delt fly) on a machine. Seated facing the pad with chest pressed against it, arms extended forward gripping the handles, moving the upper arms backward and outward in a wide arc to squeeze the rear delts and mid-back — without shrugging the shoulders. Slight bend in the elbows held constant. Front-top three-quarter camera angle showing the arms sweeping back and shoulder-blade squeeze.

**Avoid also:** shrugging shoulders up toward the ears, chest lifting off the pad, forcing the range beyond comfortable, straightening the arms.

## 06 — Seated Machine Overhead Press
`images/06-overhead-press.jpg`

**Prompt:** A fit adult performing a seated overhead press on a shoulder-press machine. Back supported against the pad, comfortable grip on the handles at shoulder height, pressing straight overhead to near extension without forcefully locking the elbows. Ribs down, no lower-back arch, neutral wrists. Side-front three-quarter camera angle showing the vertical press path and shoulder engagement.

**Avoid also:** forceful elbow lockout, excessive lower-back arch, back leaving the pad, wrists bent back.

## 07 — Seated Leg Extension
`images/07-leg-extension.jpg`

**Prompt:** A fit adult performing a seated leg extension on a machine. Seated with back against the pad, knees aligned with the machine pivot, ankle pad resting on the lower shins, extending the knees smoothly through a controlled, pain-free range with light-to-moderate load — not forcing full lockout. Hands relaxed on the side handles. Side camera angle showing the knee-extension path and quadriceps engagement.

**Avoid also:** swinging or using momentum, forced full hyperextension, knee misaligned with the pivot, gripping handles to heave.

## 08 — Hip Abductor Machine
`images/08-hip-abductor.jpg`

**Prompt:** A fit adult performing a seated hip abduction on a machine. Seated upright with back against the pad, outer thighs against the pads, opening the legs outward under smooth control to a comfortable range, then returning slowly. Torso still and upright, hands relaxed on the handles. Front camera angle showing the legs opening outward and outer-hip/glute-medius engagement.

**Avoid also:** bouncing at the end of the range, leaning the torso back to gain range, jerky motion.

`images/09-hip-adductor.jpg`

Seated upright with back against the pad, inner thighs against the pads set at a moderate, comfortable starting width, bringing the legs together slowly and under control, then returning smoothly. ## 10A — Machine Biceps Curl
`images/10a-biceps-curl.jpg`

**Prompt:** A fit adult performing a machine biceps curl. Seated with the upper arms resting on the angled pad, elbows aligned with the machine pivot, gripping the handles and curling upward through a smooth arc to full contraction, then lowering under control. Shoulders relaxed and still, no swinging. Side three-quarter camera angle showing the elbow-flexion path and biceps engagement.

**Avoid also:** shoulder movement or swinging, elbows sliding off the pad, forceful lockout at the bottom, momentum.

## 10B — Machine Triceps Extension / Cable Pressdown
`images/10b-triceps-extension.jpg`

**Prompt:** A fit adult performing a triceps cable pressdown (or machine triceps extension). Standing tall at a cable station with a straight or rope attachment, upper arms tucked close to the sides and elbows fixed, pressing the handle down to near full extension and squeezing the triceps, then returning under control. Shoulders relaxed, torso upright without leaning to add momentum. Side camera angle showing the fixed elbows and triceps engagement.

**Avoid also:** elbows drifting forward or flaring, torso rocking to push the weight, hunched shoulders.

## 11 — Pallof Press / Dead Bug
`images/11-pallof-press.jpg`

**Prompt:** A fit adult performing a standing Pallof press for anti-rotation core stability. Standing side-on to a cable machine (or resistance band anchored at chest height), gripping the handle with both hands at the chest, then pressing it straight out in front of the body and resisting the sideways pull — torso and pelvis staying square and still, ribs down, gentle brace. Front-side camera angle showing the arms extended and the stable, unrotated torso.

**Avoid also:** torso rotating toward the anchor, arching the lower back, shrugging, using momentum or speed.
